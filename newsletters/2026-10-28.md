- **Tags:** solid-electrolytes, protective-coatings, machine-learning-potentials
- **Summary:** The authors combine a PET-MAD potential fine-tuned to an r2SCAN reference dataset with experiments on Li6PS5Cl coatings. Their abstract separates adsorption control by head-group chemistry and lithiation from morphology control by chain length and coverage; intermediate chains reportedly balance moisture protection with ionic transport.
- **Why it matters:** Coating selection should consider both water exclusion and the transport penalty, rather than optimize adsorption strength alone.
