## How to interpret the enrichment

- **Evidence:** captured titles, abstracts, descriptions, and provider metadata.
  This batch uses LLM-based enrichment, not independent web verification or
  full-text reading. Source links are citations, not proof that the linked
  content was re-fetched.
- **Summary versus interpretation:** concise summaries describe the available
  source metadata; "Why it matters" is editorial analysis. Author-reported
  performance and methodological claims are not independently validated.
- **Categories and tags:** machine-learning interatomic potentials, atomistic
  chemistry/materials, agents/retrieval, mathematical methods, and possible scan
  noise are separated to make a broad scanner feed easier to browse.
- **Weak evidence:** a title-only record is not enough to establish a method's
  effectiveness. Sparse records and off-topic matches are retained with caveats.
- **Duplicates:** counts refer to saved notes, not necessarily distinct works.
  Preprint/journal versions and repeated source records remain separate; no
  historical note has been deleted or merged.
- **Preservation:** original metadata, provider summaries, and personal notes
  remain intact. Added `enrichment_*` metadata and clearly labeled AI sections
  are editorial layers, not replacements.

This is an on-demand catch-up, not a configured daily automation. See the
[repository guidance](../README.md#daily-newsletters-and-ai-enrichment) for the
coverage baseline and next-edition procedure.
