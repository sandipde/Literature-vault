# LONG-RANGE 155 MM ARTILLERY PROPELLING CHARGE DEVELOPMENT WITH A NEW HIGH-ENERGY LOVA PROPELLANT COMPOSITION

## Summary
Since the start of the war in Ukraine, one can realize the emphasis put on long-range artillery fires. Based on this observation, EURENCO is developing new artillery propelling charges with improved range. A new HE-LOVA composition has been developed based on a qualified EURENCO LOVA composition and industrial process. The main components are RDX, nitrocellulose and a mix of energetic plasticizers. Besides, a new supercharge design allows to pack more propellant. The HE-LOVA propellant exhibits increased impetus (+12,5 %) compared to the M30 multibase propellant. Using numerical modelling, optimal dimensions for the propellant were determined. Prototype supercharges were manufactured and then fired at the Bofors Test Center. Performances are improved compared to standard Z6 with velocities up to 1069 m/s. External ballistics calculations have been performed and the maximum range is estimated at over 50,5 km (+26%) for a base-bleed projectile and over 41,5 km for a hollow-base projectile (+30%).
