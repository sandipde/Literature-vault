# Machine Learning the $GW$ Self-Energy Operator for Real Materials

- **Type:** arxiv
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.20247v1
- **Captured by:** @sandipde
- **Metadata:** complete (arXiv)
- **Authors:** Xian Xu, Bowen Hou, Chengyan Zhang, Diana Y. Qiu
- **Published:** 2026-11-15

## Summary
Machine learning of $GW$ in solids has largely focused on prediction of scalar quasiparticle energy corrections. Here, we introduce a phase-aware many-body transformer that predicts the full $\mathbf{k}$-resolved complex self-energy matrix. As a proof of principle, we apply the model to predict quasiparticle energies before and after diagonalization of the quasiparticle Hamiltonian, obtaining a test set mean absolute error (MAE) of 29 meV for a dataset of 1745 2D materials in the static COHSEX approximation and 60 meV for a more limited dataset of 433 materials calculated within a plasmon-pole model. COHSEX pretraining followed by finetuning reduces this MAE to 42 meV. The predicted matrices also accurately reproduce QP wavefunctions.
