# Orientation-Tunable Interfacial Thermal Conductance in Lateral MoSe2/MoS2 Heterostructures: A Deep Potential Molecular Dynamics Study

- **Type:** arxiv
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.24547
- **Captured by:** @auto-scanner
- **Metadata:** complete (arXiv)
- **Authors:** Shirin Sabokdast, Pedram Mirchi, Ali Rajabpour
- **Published:** 2026-11-18

## Summary
Managing heat flow across interfaces in lateral 2D transition-metal dichalcogenide (TMD) heterostructures is a key challenge for next-generation nanoelectronic and optoelectronic devices. To tackle this, we trained a deep learning interatomic potential (DLP) using DeePMD-kit on density functional theory (DFT) data, then paired it with non-equilibrium molecular dynamics (NEMD) simulations to study interfacial thermal conductance (ITC) in lateral MoSe$_2$/MoS$_2$ heterostructures, considering both armchair and zigzag interface orientations. The potential we trained closely matches DFT energies, forces, and phonon dispersion relations, which confirms its reliability for thermal transport simulations. Examining cell lengths from 10 to 40 nm and extrapolating to infinite length, the armchair interface shows a higher ITC (473 MW/m$^2$K) than the zigzag interface (387 MW/m$^2$K), and the armchair interface remains higher at every length considered. Both increase further as temperature rises. At 300 K, the vibrational density of states shows greater phonon spectral overlap at the armchair interface than at the zigzag interface. Together, these results offer a solid machine-learning framework for studying heat transport in lateral TMD heterostructures, and identify interface orientation as an effective means of controlling heat dissipation at these junctions.
