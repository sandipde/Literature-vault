## Summary
Abstract The Age Universal Intrinsic-Extrinsic Religious Orientation Scale contains two separate subscales designed to measure two distinct religious orientations: an intrinsic (I) and an extrinsic (E) orientation. But, as the name suggests, this scale is useful for both adults and children. Since its publication in 1983, the scale has been revised based on further psychometric evaluation to a 14-item scale (Religious Orientation Scale–Revised; Gorsuch & McPherson, 1989). In addition, Maltby (1999) has provided a 12-item version of the scale. It should be noted that the 1989 revised scale identified two extrinsic orientations: the Extrinsic Personal orientation and the Extrinsic Social orientation. Some of the research using this 1983 scale has also made that distinction, though the distinction itself was not identified in the original publication.
