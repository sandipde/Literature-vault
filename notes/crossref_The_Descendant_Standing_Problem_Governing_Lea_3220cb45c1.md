## Summary
Adaptive and agentic AI systems increasingly learn from outputs that are themselves products of governance. Approvals, denials, verifier decisions, reviewer judgments, eligibility findings, and related determinations may become labels, rewards, preferences, examples, memory entries, or other supervisory signals. This creates a downstream governance problem when a determination that has already entered a learning lineage is later classified, under admissible retrospective review, as having lacked warrant under the historical conditions governing it when issued. This paper formalizes the Descendant Standing Problem: the assessment of a learned descendant's currently asserted governing basis after retrospective same-rule warrant failure in its governance-learning lineage. The framework separates recorded provenance, causal learning descent, and justificatory support; distinguishes ordinary standing, operative use, and defect-relative independent standing; and represents asserted governing bases with finite AND-OR/Horn support structures. Clean re-derivation is three-valued and distinguishes positive route completeness from negative witness-space closure, preventing incomplete provenance from being treated as either independence or defeat. The paper does not claim novelty for truth maintenance, Horn closure, provenance, machine unlearning, recursive taint, independent-source reasoning, or policy-feedback effects. Its contribution is the governance object to which those mechanisms are coupled. The formal core introduces set-relative warrant-withdrawal semantics for support dependence; a conditional semantic correctness theorem under the stated currentsupport-audit, normal-form, rule-soundness, label-correctness, semantic-coverage, and certificate-soundness premises; an influence-status object distinct from materiality; and a binary realizability result showing that resolved causal influence status and materiality cannot universally determine independent standing. A synthetic lineage case shows how a fresh evaluator may still rely on lineage-shaped evidence through selection, observability, labels, thresholds, or calibration. The resulting remediation architecture keeps source correction, causal repair, current admission, operative disposition, and downstream decision review claim-relative and distinct under the stated non-entailment relations.
