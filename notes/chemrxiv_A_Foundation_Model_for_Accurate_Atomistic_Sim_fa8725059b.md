# A Foundation Model for Accurate Atomistic Simulations in Drug Design

## Summary
Artificial intelligence has revolutionized the prediction of static protein structures, but characterizing their dynamics and their interactions with drug candidates remains a computational bottleneck. Simulations that address this challenge need both the accuracy of quantum chemistry and the speed to reach biologically relevant timescales. To meet these requirements, we introduce FeNNix-Bio1, a fast and scalable foundation machine-learning potential trained primarily on high-accuracy quantum chemistry data, which enables reactive atomistic simulations of biological systems. Here, we show that FeNNix-Bio1 captures subtle condensed-phase phenomena such as ion solvation and the properties of liquid water, predicts the hydration free energies of more than 600 small molecules and the absolute binding free energies of 30 protein-ligand complexes in good agreement with experiment, and describes protein structure and dynamics, lipid bilayers and chemical reactions, in simulations of up to more than a million atoms.
