## Summary
How far apart, on average, can $n$ points in a set $A$ be? As $n\to\infty$ the answer is the $\textit{maximal energy}$ $M^{+}(A)$, the largest possible value of $\mathbb{E}|X-Y|$ when $X$ and $Y$ are independent random points of $A$ with a common distribution. If the distribution is allowed to carry negative mass, we obtain a second constant $M(A)\ge M^{+}(A)$.
