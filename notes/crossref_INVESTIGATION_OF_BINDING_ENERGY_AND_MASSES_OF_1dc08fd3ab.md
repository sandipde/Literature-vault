## Summary
This work investigates the binding energies and mass spectra of heavy baryons within the quark–diquark framework. In this approach, two correlated quarks are treated as an effective diquark, reducing the three-body problem to a two-body quark–diquark system. The calculation is carried out in two stages. First, the diquark masses are obtained by solving the corresponding two-quark bound-state problem with an appropriate quark–quark interaction potential. The baryon masses are then determined from the interaction between the remaining quark and the diquark. The total baryon mass is expressed in terms of the constituent quark mass, diquark mass, and quark–diquark binding energy. Coulomb-plus-linear potentials including spin-dependent terms are employed to describe the internal dynamics. The formalism is applied to charm and bottom baryons, including doubly and triply heavy states, and provides results suitable for comparison with other theoretical approaches and available experimental data.
