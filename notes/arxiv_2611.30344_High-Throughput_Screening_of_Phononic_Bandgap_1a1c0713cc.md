# High-Throughput Screening of Phononic Bandgap in Experimentally Realized Materials

## Summary
Materials with a phonon band gap offer a unique mechanism to restrict phonon scattering, making them promising candidates for developing functional materials for targeted thermal applications. Unlocking this mechanism across broad material classes requires systematic exploration, but first-principles methods and experiments are too expensive. Here, we used the universal machine-learning interatomic potential to quantify phonons and thermodynamic properties. We validated the accuracy of machine-learning-calculated results by comparing them with density-functional theory. We screened 154,879 materials and performed phonon calculations for 42,347 thermodynamically stable and experimentally observed compounds. We then performed phonon band gap analysis for dynamically stable materials and identified 11,176 materials with a phononic gap, including 10,960 materials with optical-optical gaps and 270 with acoustic-optical gaps. We also analyzed the lattice thermal conductivity of materials with phonon band gaps to identify candidates with acoustic-optical gaps and low thermal conductivity. Finally, by solving the linearized phonon Boltzmann equation from first principles, we demonstrate low room-temperature lattice thermal conductivities in three Li-metal binaries.
