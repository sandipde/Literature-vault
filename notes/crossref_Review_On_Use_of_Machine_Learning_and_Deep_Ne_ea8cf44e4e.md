## Summary
Cyber-attacks have become a significant concern for society, affecting industries, corporate environments, and personal devices. With the rapid evolution of the internet, our connectivity through networked devices has increased, making robust network security a critical area of research. Detecting anomalies in network traffic is essential for effective network security, as it can reveal potential threats. Developing Anomaly Detection system with machine learning and deep neural network techniques offers a comprehensive approach to safeguarding networks. Anomalies within networks and systems are a growing concern, and our focus remains on advancing systems that can accurately detect and respond to abnormal network activity.
