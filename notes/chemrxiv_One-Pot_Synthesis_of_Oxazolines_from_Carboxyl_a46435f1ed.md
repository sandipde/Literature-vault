## Summary
Oxazolines are important heterocycles for a range of industrial applications, and yet their synthesis from simple carboxylic acid and amino alcohol substrates requires multiple steps and stoichiometric reagents. Here, we demonstrate the one-pot direct synthesis using a bifunctional boronic acid/phosphorus(V) catalyst that is shown to catalyze both the amidation and cyclodehydration steps. Different classes of carboxylic acid coupling partners are surveyed, the mechanism for cyclodehydration investigated, and a variety of 2-substituted oxazolines prepared without the addition of stoichiometric reagents.
