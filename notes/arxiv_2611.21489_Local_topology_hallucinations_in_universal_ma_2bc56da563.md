# Local topology hallucinations in universal machine-learned interatomic potentials

- **Type:** arxiv
- **Source:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21489

## Summary
Predicting metastable states with universal machine-learned interatomic potentials requires that they preserve the minima and saddles of the reference potential-energy surface. Here we identify local topology hallucination, in which distinct model minima relax into the same reference basin. This failure arises from mode-selective curvature-sign errors shared by MACE, CHGNet and M3GNet. Curvature signs predicted by two of the three models agreed with density-functional calculations in 55 of 57 positive but only 5 of 56 negative cases. In LiGe$_2$S, two MACE minima connected by a verified saddle relaxed into one density-functional basin. Reference relaxation likewise returned nine of ten selected model minima to their reference basins. A directional test with two displaced reference calculations rejected 9 of 12 prospectively tested model instabilities. Model agreement alone therefore cannot establish that predicted minima are distinct metastable states.
