# Multistate Potential Energy Surfaces from an Equivariant Core-Hamiltonian Correction to Semiempirical Configuration Interaction

## Summary
Nonadiabatic dynamics needs several coupled potential energy surfaces at once. Fitting each state separately discards the electronic structure that couples them, whereas semiempirical configuration interaction in the style of the orthogonalization-corrected OMx methods obtains all states from one Hamiltonian and thereby keeps the topology of their intersections. We propose to build multistate surfaces by learning inside this Hamiltonian. An equivariant network predicts a geometry-dependent correction to the one-electron core Hamiltonian, the operator that already carries the orthogonalization corrections of OMx methods, while the two-electron integrals keep their NDDO form. Configuration interaction then yields all states, their gaps, and analytic forces from the corrected operator, and the whole model is trained end to end on reference state energies. For 1,3-cyclohexadiene with XMS-CASPT2 references, the correction lowers the three-state error of refitted ODM3 from 0.222 to 0.096 eV on held-out geometries and halves the error of every state and gap. Along a new nonadiabatic trajectory propagated on the corrected surfaces, it agrees with XMS-CASPT2 to 0.09 eV, compared with 0.46 eV for published ODM3.
