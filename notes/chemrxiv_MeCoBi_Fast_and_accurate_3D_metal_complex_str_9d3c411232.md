# MeCoBi: Fast and accurate 3D metal complex structures for structure-based machine learning

- **Published:** 2026-11-17

## Summary
The three-dimensional arrangement of ligands around a metal centre determines its electronic structure, reactivity, and biological activity, yet generating accurate 3D structures from a string-based input remains difficult. Existing tools are slow, narrow in coverage, or need the coordination environment to be specified by hand. We present MeCoBi (Metal Complex Builder), an automated pipeline that builds and optimises 3D structures of mononuclear metal complexes from ligand SMILES across 36 d-block and main-group metals and coordination numbers 2–9. On 30,830 experimental structures from the Cambridge Structural Database, MeCoBi generated a structure for 99.6% of complexes with a median error in metal–ligand bond lengths of 0.06 Å. Used as machine-learning inputs, these structures ranked the untested members of an in-house library of 1,800 iridium complexes at least as well as the best 2D models (area under the receiver operating characteristic curve 0.88 against at most 0.86); and, through GFN2-xTB single points, lowered the error of a two-dimensional model for the dipole moments and HOMO–LUMO gaps of 10,175 tmQM complexes from 1.68 to 1.24 D and from 0.367 to 0.311 eV. MeCoBi is open source, with a web interface at mecobi.york.ac.uk.
