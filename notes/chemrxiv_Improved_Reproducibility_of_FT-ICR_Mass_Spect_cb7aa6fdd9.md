## Summary
Peak reproducibility is a major factor that affects the reliability of Fourier transform ion cyclotron resonance mass spectrometry (FT-ICR MS) spectra of dissolved organic matter (DOM), which was addressed by a newly proposed workflow, namely parallel samples representation by similar samples (PSRSS). The performance of the workflow was validated by parallel measurements and experimental replicates. Inspection of isotopic and homologous peaks in the PSRSS workflow enabled elimination of electronic noise peaks and low-abundance peaks with few occurrences, significantly decreased the Bray-Curtis dissimilarity, and improved the molecular formula assignment ratio with only minor effects on the assigned formulae. The Bray-Curtis dissimilarity threshold (55% to 60% or quartile) was proposed to cluster FT-ICR MS spectra for DOM samples with similar molecular composition. The noise peak removal performance was further improved for the similar molecular composition samples by excluding outlier spectra and less frequently occurring peaks via the PSRSS workflow. Moreover, the reliability of DOM molecular characterization was significantly improved by the PSRSS workflow. Overall, our findings demonstrated the feasibility of the PSRSS workflow to serve as a reliable, labor-saving method to remove electronic noise peaks and cluster spectra, yielding reliable spectral 3information for multiple independent FT-ICR MS spectra.
