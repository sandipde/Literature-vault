# Accuracy and Limits of Pretrained Interatomic Potentials Across Molecular Reaction Pathways

- **Published:** 2026-11-23

## Summary
Foundation models of machine-learning interatomic potentials are now used to search for reaction pathways, but the common reactive test sets share structures with the training data for several of these models. We compare energies and forces from fifteen pretrained models with DFT single-point values at fixed GFN2-xTB-derived geometries from 9,017 gas-phase reaction pathways in DORTS-9K, a separately generated dataset, and extend the comparison to seventeen models for the reactions that contain only carbon, hydrogen, nitrogen, and oxygen (CHNO). Models trained on molecular data are the most accurate, and the best of them, UMA-M OMol, has a median force RMSE of 0.018 eV Å⁻¹ and reproduces 96% of the 3,713 CHNO barriers within 1 kcal mol⁻¹. Force errors increase from the endpoints to the transition-state anchors for all molecular models, nearly doubling for UMA-M OMol. Across the 38 CHNO reactions with the largest UMA-M OMol barrier errors, all models except AIMNet2 and AIMNet2-RXN underestimate the DFT barriers by 6.5–85.2% across models and reactions. These include hydrogen transfers involving nitrous acid and an azide/isocyanate template.
