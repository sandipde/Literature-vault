## Summary
Science Education can be described as transaction of scientific knowledge in the classroom situation aims in developing leaners knowledge, comprehension of scientific concepts, their application in real life situation, inculcating scientific skills and attitudes, temper and habits among the learners. To achieve these aims, pedagogy of science must be based on the principle of “Learning by doing” and “Minds-on” experiences. One of the most appropriate teaching-learning approaches that strictly follows the principle of “Learning by doing’ is Constructivist Approach. This particular study emphasises on exploring the application of Constructivist Approach in teaching science to develop deeper understanding of scientific facts, theories and principles. It also throughs light on role of teacher and the taught and appropriate techniques of assessment prescribe by constructivism as a basic principle in Science Education. Various related research literature is reviewed to comprehend the related issues and to reveal the expectation of the current era of revolution from teaching and constructing learning. Current trends of globalised education system demand technologically sound application-based and experiential learning. It also demands providing opportunities for scientific study and creativity within a global context which will stimulate and challenge the learner. Constructivism has emerged as one of the best solutions to serve these purposes.
