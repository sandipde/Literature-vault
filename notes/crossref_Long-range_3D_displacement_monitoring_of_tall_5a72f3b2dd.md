# Long-range 3D displacement monitoring of tall structures using a wireless ultra-wide-baseline stereo vision system

## Summary
Long-range three-dimensional (3D) displacement monitoring of tall structures requires a wide stereo baseline, which introduces challenges in synchronisation, data transmission, and calibration. This study develops a wireless ultra-wide-baseline stereo vision system for target-free structural monitoring. Two independently deployed cameras are synchronised using GNSS-based hardware triggering and track natural features locally, transmitting only pixel coordinates for cloud-based 3D reconstruction. A field calibration method combines surveyed camera geometry with rotation refinement using known structural dimensions. Validation tests demonstrated synchronisation errors below 100 μs and displacement measurement errors of 0.58 mm and 1.87 mm in two horizontal directions at approximately 190 m. The system was further applied to a 229 m tall guyed mast using 296 m and 48 m baselines. The wider baseline provided stronger depth sensitivity and more balanced measurement quality. These results demonstrate the feasibility of wireless, target-free 3D displacement monitoring of tall structures.
